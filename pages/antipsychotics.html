Typical Antipsychotics
First-Generation (FGAs)
D2 receptor antagonists. Classified by potency — high potency drugs carry greater EPS risk; low potency carry greater autonomic side effects.
High potency: Haloperidol, Fluphenazine — Low potency: Chlorpromazine, Thioridazine

Atypical Antipsychotics
Second-Generation (SGAs)
Block D2 and 5-HT2A receptors. Lower EPS risk but associated with metabolic syndrome. Clozapine reserved for treatment-resistant cases.
Clozapine, Risperidone, Olanzapine, Quetiapine, Aripiprazole, Ziprasidone & more
