Antidepressants by Class

Organized following the USMLE Step 1 framework. Select a class to browse its drugs.

🌞
Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors
SSRIs
First-line antidepressants. Block serotonin reuptake transporter (SERT).
Fluoxetine, Sertraline, Escitalopram, Citalopram, Paroxetine, Fluvoxamine
▶ 6 drugs available
🌞
Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitors
SNRIs
Block both SERT and NET. Used for depression, anxiety, and pain syndromes.
Venlafaxine, Duloxetine, Desvenlafaxine, Levomilnacipran, Milnacipran
▶ 5 drugs available
🌞
Tricyclic Antidepressants
TCAs
Block SERT, NET and muscarinic/histamine receptors. High toxicity in overdose.
Amitriptyline, Nortriptyline, Imipramine, Clomipramine, Doxepin
Coming soon
🌞
Monoamine Oxidase Inhibitors
MAOIs
Inhibit MAO-A/MAO-B, increasing monoamine levels. Significant dietary interactions.
Phenelzine, Tranylcypromine, Isocarboxazid, Selegiline (patch)
Coming soon
🌞
Atypical Antidepressants
Varied mechanisms not fitting the other classes. Used for depression, smoking cessation, and insomnia.
Bupropion, Mirtazapine, Trazodone, Vilazodone, Vortioxetine
