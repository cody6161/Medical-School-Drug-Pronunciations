# 💊 Medical School Drug Pronunciation Guide

> Audio pronunciations for drugs across every major pharmacology category — built for medical students.

**🌐 Live Site → [cody6161.github.io/Medical-School-Drug-Pronunciations](https://cody6161.github.io/Medical-School-Drug-Pronunciations/)**

---

## What It Is

A free, browser-based audio reference for hearing the correct pronunciation of drugs. No login, no app — just click a drug card and hit play.

Built to cover the full medical school pharmacology curriculum as it grows.

---

## How to Use

1. Visit the **[live site](https://cody6161.github.io/Medical-School-Drug-Pronunciations/)**
2. Select a drug category from the home page
3. Find the drug you want — click the **play button** on its card to hear the pronunciation
4. Works on desktop and mobile

---
